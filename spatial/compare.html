How to read the measurements

F is the finished footprint and A is recorded assembly activity. Solid lines trace the visible form. A dashed segment on V29 marks the approximate edge behind the robot. Each role pie divides its recorded area by A. Role areas can overlap. The Top Views show recorded movement coverage. For access and clearance, check the full assembly sequence.

The bars in the finished member row show each case’s share of the pair’s total for that metric. Dimensions follow the Rhino axes. Member surface area includes contact faces. ≈ marks a rounded model dimension or an area scaled from a published dimension.

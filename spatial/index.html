How to read the areas

A is the saved total assembly activity area. F is the finished object’s plan footprint. The three role areas can overlap, so coverage percentages can total more than 100%. Group medians describe the five recorded cases in each assembly mode. Ratios are dimensionless. The V27 footprint uses a rod projection.

The highlighted track is the selected role’s largest saved straight-line displacement from start to end in plan coordinates. These points locate a recorded movement. Design questions about access, clearance, or handoff call for checking the full sequence.

How far does part movement extend beyond the finished object?

Put the finished object and the part tracks on the same plan. Their combined area divided by the finished footprint is E/F. At 1×, all tracked part coverage fits within the finished footprint.

Read both charts together. V30 has an E/F of 36.19×, while V01 has a larger combined area (28.0 m² vs 8.6 m²). Use the area to judge the scale of the layout and the ratio to see how much it expands relative to the finished object.

How cases are paired

Footprints are similar when they differ by at most 20% of the larger value. Total activity and part coverage are similar within 15%. A large difference in activity means the larger value is at least 1.5 times the smaller. Each question shows selected examples.

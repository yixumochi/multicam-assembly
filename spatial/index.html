Method

F is the area of up-facing faces of the authored solid, rasterized at 1 cm for millimetre models and 2 cm for a model-unit file. M is the union of convex hulls of the saved part-track points after they were fitted onto that plan. E is the union of those two shapes. A hull is the spread of the lifted points, not a swept solid and not a buffered body. V07 and V21 stay in model units and are not plotted with the metre cases. Tracks are not split into Transport, Reorientation, Interface Operation, or Withdrawal.

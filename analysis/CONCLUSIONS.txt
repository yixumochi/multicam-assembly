Conclusions from the handoff snapshot

Observation: The export retained all 431 pairs, with cosine scores from 0.50 to 1.00. Of these, 91 scored at least 0.65 and 340 scored below 0.65. The angle plot uses 240 pairs and the grip plot uses 190. Pairs with missing geometry remain in the text comparison.

Observation: Pair 3 has a text cosine of 0.897, with recorded start-to-end angle changes of 27.5 and -2.9 degrees. Pair 107 has a cosine of 0.638. The magnitudes of its angle changes differ by only 0.1 degrees, while its recorded starting angles are 16.9 and -54.1 degrees. Pair 358 has a cosine of 0.519 and different recorded angles and grip descriptions across the two exit labels.

Scope: This is an exploratory comparison of operations across cases. The differences motivate questions about gripping, support, entry directions, and clearance during withdrawal. These requirements were not measured in this snapshot.

Interpretation: Language scores help select operations for side-by-side review. The identification of equivalent operations requires further evidence. A close geometric comparison alone cannot validate the pairing.

Design questions: Pair 3 concerns support at the joint and the directions available for adjustment. Pair 107 concerns differences in approach and grip position. Pair 358 concerns withdrawal space and release conditions near the joint. These questions await video review.

Items to verify: Original video keyframes, the angle reference axis and camera scale, and the definitions of grip and left_px. This page uses the wheel arcs and the exported angle and grip records.

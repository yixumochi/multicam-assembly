Type 2 · how to read five plates

Type 1 follows the member. Here the member is aligned and the labeled hand or robot is compared at a (start), b (reference), c (end). g/H is the saved grip travel relative to b, normalized by the recorded H in pixels; it is not a world distance. The public export does not give a reproducible landmark rule for H.

The middle zero is by construction, not a pause. In V01, V04 and V21 all three outlines reach the image edge. Their spans describe only the visible body, not the whole actor.
